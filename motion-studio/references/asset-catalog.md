### Map beat `# sshape` (engine/library/s_shape.js)
OverSimplified colour-block map of Việt Nam (Đại Việt red with a face, Champa teal, Khmer violet; Hoàng Sa & Trường Sa always shown).
`mode=grow stages="938;Independence|1069;…"` (one stage per caption; ids today blob 938 1069 1306 1471 1611 1653 1697 1698 1757) ·
`mode=terrain` (Trường Sơn rises, Biển Đông, rice arrows south) · `mode=narrow` (≈50 km waist near Đồng Hới) · `mode=length`
(1,650 km ruler + train line) · `face=yes mood=grin|shock|sad` · `s=yes` (golden S at "today") · `label= sub=`.
Hats added: `crown`, `mandarin` (mũ cánh chuồn) for kings and officials.

### Flat-3D sets — real camera angles, flat animation (engine/library/flat3d.js + hanoi_set.js + shots_ep4.js)
`# moment kind=street3d` on a fully built Hà Nội Old Quarter street (tube houses, shopfronts, signs in Vietnamese, balconies,
plants, flags, parked scooters, stools, wires, trees, lamps, zebra crossing, traffic). Keys: `cam=eye|worm|crane|close|side|high|ots|chase`
or `path="x,y,z,yaw,pitch,fov@t|…"`, `push= rise=`, `time=dusk|day`, `traffic=`, `speed=`, `ev=yes`, `dave="x,z"` `pose=` `expr=`,
`walk="t0-t1:x0>x1" mode=steady|run|stop`, `flow=yes` (traffic bends round Dave), `hand=yes`, `done=t` (cheer), `copho="x,z"`,
`bus=t`, `near=t` (scooter whips past the lens + shake), `title="BIG|small" titleAt=` (art-directed lettering), `lb=no`.
Build new sets the same way: F3.cam/begin/quad/box/line/billboard/flush, F3.onQuad to paint signs and windows on facades,
F3.path for camera moves. Also `shot_bird` `shot_eyes` `shot_worm` `shot_ots` `shot_street` (engine/library/cine_shots.js).

### Real 3D set pieces (engine/library/scene3d.js — three.js, ~1 frame/s at 1080p: use for the big shots)
`# moment kind=map3d stages="938;Independence|1306;…|today;"` — Việt Nam as a raised 3D relief; Đại Việt rises era by era,
camera glides north → south, solid 3D year titles, Trường Sơn peaks, Hoàng Sa & Trường Sa shown.
`# moment kind=fleet3d` — 1306 wedding fleet on a moving sea at golden hour, fog, mountains, lanterns, crane-up, 3D "1306".
Build new 3D shots the same way: `renderer()`, a cached scene per kind, `title3d()` for solid 3D lettering, composite with drawImage.
2D cinematic set pieces: `wow_fleet wow_storm wow_south wow_morph` (engine/library/wow_concepts.js).
