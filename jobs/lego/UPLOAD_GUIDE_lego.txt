=== 2. MÔ TẢ (copy nguyên khối dưới đây) ===
LEGO nearly died in 2004, but it never went bankrupt. It lost count of what its own sets cost, sold some boxes for less than the parts inside, and was saved by counting. Here is how, from LEGO's own annual reports.

Everyone says LEGO almost went bankrupt and was saved by going back to the brick. The record says something stranger: in its worst year the toy business still made a profit before write-downs; the giant loss was clean-up; the parts library had doubled to more than 12,000 pieces, most used in a single set; and the rescue was a cost system, a cut to about 7,000 parts, and an 800-million-kroner loan from the family's holding company, with interest. Why LEGO almost failed in 2003 and 2004, who Jørgen Vig Knudstorp is, what the complexity tax is, and how LEGO made its comeback.
