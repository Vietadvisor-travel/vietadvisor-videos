On Wednesday 21 October 2026, two documents about the same company came out on the same day. In California, Meta reported $64.6 billion of revenue in three months. In Santa Fe, a New Mexico judge ordered it to pay $25 billion. Everyone says Meta was finally punished for Cambridge Analytica.
