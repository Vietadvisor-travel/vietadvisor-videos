=== 2. MÔ TẢ (copy nguyên khối dưới đây) ===
On 2 October 2026 Nike's stock touched $31.97, an intraday low not seen in 13 years. Three weeks later Adidas reported the first billion-euro quarter in its history. Everyone says Adidas finally beat Nike. The record says something else: that quarter Adidas's footwear sales were flat. Nike took its own shoes off the shelf, and Adidas sold the shoes it already had.

Why is Nike failing? From the Dassler brothers' split in 1948 and Bowerman's waffle iron, to the 1984 Michael Jordan bet and the shoe that was never banned, to Consumer Direct Acceleration in 2020 (when Nike walked away from stores like Belk, Dillard's and Zappos), the Yeezy collapse, the Samba comeback, the 2026 World Cup ad war and Nike's Investor Day: how a sales channel decided the Nike vs Adidas rivalry.
