# Brand Rules (Vietadvisor)

Distilled from the Vietadvisor social & brand system. These override creative preferences.

## Voice
A knowledgeable local friend — specific, honest, warm, sometimes witty. Not a tour guide reading a
script, not a tourism board, not an influencer performing excitement, not a salesperson.

- **Specific**: "~45,000 ₫ (~$1.80) at this stall, cash" — never "affordable local food".
- **Honest**: "Avoid weekends — crowded. Tuesday morning is the moment."
- **Local**: Vietnamese words with an immediate gloss: "bánh mì (baguette sandwich)", "xe ôm (motorbike taxi)".
- Prices: always VND **and** approximate USD, plus the month checked ("checked Oct 2026").
- Max one exclamation mark per video's captions; no ALL-CAPS shouting in captions (titles/stamps are fine).

## Banned phrases
"hidden gem", "bucket list", "must-visit before you die", "paradise on earth", "wanderlust",
"nestled in", "boasts", "a testament to", "vibrant tapestry", any superlative we can't defend
("the best phở in Vietnam").

## Save test
Every video must include at least one: a real price with date · a time/season recommendation ·
a route/logistics detail · an honest warning · a local alternative to the famous spot.

## Social language
Social output is **English only** (other languages are for the website/long-form localisations).
Captions are English; on-screen place names keep Vietnamese diacritics.

## Video rules
- Frame 1 has motion and text. No fade-in, no slow drone opener, no logo intro.
- Design for sound-off: all information readable without audio.
- 9:16 safe zone: keep all text in the middle 60 % vertically (y 0.2–0.8).
- Brand colour `#EE4D2D` only on emphasis: numbers, prices, punchline word, CTA.
- End every video with the end frame: "— Vietadvisor" on the brand background (1.5–2 s).
- Short-form sweet spot: 7–20 s for Reels formats A/B/C/D; explainer episodes 60–120 s; long-form via chapters.

## Facts & sensitivity
- Map of Vietnam always shows Hoàng Sa and Trường Sa (the engine draws them).
- Ethnic minority characters appear as hosts, experts, neighbours — dress simplified but accurate
  (Black H'Mông indigo + silver rings; Flower H'Mông colour bands; Red Dao red turban with tassels;
  White Thái silver butterfly buttons + khăn piêu). Never a punchline about culture or appearance.
- Safety: riders wear helmets; alcohol scenes always carry the zero-alcohol-limit / no-riding note;
  dangerous viewpoints → "pull over to look".
- Never fabricate prices, opening hours or rules. Cite sources in `EPISODE.sources`.


## Photos & stickers (mixed media)
- Only: Vietadvisor's own photos, photos the user supplies with rights, or free-licence images
  (Unsplash / Pexels licence, CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA, public domain) — always with `--credit` and `--license`.
- Never: stock photos without a licence, social-media photos, photos of identifiable private people
  without consent, logos or branded products as the hero of a shot.
- Credits appear on photo plates and in `out/<slug>_credits.txt` — paste them into the video description.
