# Story archetypes — pick the shape, then the film writes itself

`node tools/pp_new.mjs <slug> "<Subject>" --archetype <key>` builds the 7 act files with these act names, colours and timings
(10 min default; `--min 8…12`). Every archetype keeps the channel signature: cold open → `# ppcase` → acts → thesis on black → end card.

## How to use an archetype without becoming formulaic
- Keep the shape, change the camera: never open two episodes with the same recipe (rotate S01 / S26 / S36 / S27 as cold opens).
- One device is the episode's **motif** (the Quibi phone, the Kodak film roll, the Theranos nanotainer) — introduce it in a1, transform it in a4, pay it in a6.
- The lesson is about people, not companies; end on a sentence the viewer can apply to their own work.
